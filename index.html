What it does

Designed to disappear into your day.

Three things, done properly. No streak anxiety, no forty-tab settings screen, no food database archaeology.

Snap a meal

Photograph your plate and Café Cal estimates the calories and macros. Tap any number to correct it. It learns the foods you eat often.

Read your day

One screen: calories on top, protein, carbs and fat beneath, each in its own colour. You can read where you stand in about a second.

Build the habit

Gentle streaks, weekly insights and Thali tiers reward showing up. Miss a day and nothing shouts at you. The app just carries on.

Your day at a glance

Four numbers. One look.

Every nutrient keeps the same colour everywhere in the app: on the ring, on the tile, in the chart, on the share card. You learn it once.

Privacy

Your data is yours.

Café Cal stores what it needs to make the app work, and nothing more. Leaving is a first-class feature, not a support ticket.

One sign-in

Google or email, your choice. Your meals, weight and goals are never sold, rented or shared with advertisers.

Anonymous diagnostics

Crash reports are tied to a device install ID rather than your account, and are kept for up to 90 days to fix bugs.

Delete anytime

Remove your account and everything in it from inside the app in one tap, or by email if you can't get in.
